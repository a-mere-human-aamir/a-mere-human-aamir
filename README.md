# a mere human — Aamir, human

**Helping people live easier lives.**

I’m Aamir—an engineer, founder, and systems architect working at the intersection of enterprise infrastructure, governed AI autonomy, and accessible innovation.

Over the years, my work has spanned financial technology, telecommunications, connected mobility, and research-driven ventures. The common thread is simple: technology should make complex things easier for people, not put more barriers in their way.

## What I’m building

### [Flockrush Grid](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://grid_flockrush.ar.io/)

An **OS for Autonomy®** designed to coordinate specialized AI agents through a Policy Mesh, with verifiable governance and human oversight. My aim is to bring sophisticated operational capabilities within reach of more people and organizations.

### [Surfable](https://surfable.ca/)

Advanced battery R&D and AI-assisted materials exploration, focused on building a more resilient and accessible energy future.

### [Refiction](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://refiction_flockrush.ar.io/)

**When Reality Outruns Fiction** — a serialized technology thriller about invention, sovereignty, resilience, and the people behind the systems.

## What I care about

* **Human agency:** People should set the intent and retain meaningful authority.
* **Resilient systems:** Build for recovery, accountability, and continuity.
* **Access to innovation:** Reduce the technical and economic barriers to getting things done.
* **Engineering that lasts:** Make systems reliable enough to outlive a single tool, vendor, or generation of hardware.
